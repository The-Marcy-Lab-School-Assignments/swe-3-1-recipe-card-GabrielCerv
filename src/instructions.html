Chicken Alfredo Pasta

| Homepage | | Ingredients Page | | Instructions |

This dish is crafted using rich Fettuccine Cheese, Pasta Noodles, Creamy Alfredo Sauce, and Premium Chicken

Instructions

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until it is tender but not too soft,
    then drain and set it aside.
  2. Season the chicken with salt and pepper on both sides.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and cook the chicken until it is golden and fully cooked,
    then cut it into bite sized pieces.
  4. In the same pan, melt butter and cook the garlic for about a minute until it smells good.
  5. Pour in the cream, add Parmesan cheese, and stir until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy
  6. Add the cooked pasta and chicken to the pan and mix until everything is well coated with sauce.
  7. Taste the pasta, add more salt or pepper if needed, and serve while it is hot.
